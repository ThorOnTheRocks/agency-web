This project reframes my website around a clearer commercial promise. The goal was to keep the editorial taste and technical rigor while making the experience faster to understand for founders and product teams considering a launch, redesign, or rescue engagement.

## Focus

- Sharper offer positioning in the first viewport
- Earlier proof and stronger client-case framing
- Selective contact qualification instead of a generic portfolio form
- Fewer developer-adjacent cues in navigation, footer, and content presentation

## Stack

-   **Astro**: For static site generation and island architecture.
-   **React**: For interactive components.
-   **Tailwind CSS**: For rapid styling.
-   **Framer Motion**: For smooth animations.
